FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I book a manicure or pedicure?

The quickest way is online through the Notino Salóny booking system — you can see available appointments in real time and choose your nail technician too. You can also book by phone on +421 944 328 358 or by message on Instagram @atmosfera.nails.ba.

Where is the salon located and where can I park?

You will find us at Prešovská 40/B, Bratislava – Ružinov, on the ground floor on the inner side of the block. Parking is available nearby in line with the City of Bratislava parking policy.

How long does gel polish last?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks. We recommend renewing gel polish every 2 to 3 weeks — that way your nails stay healthy and your manicure looks neat. For weak or brittle nails, we recommend an IBX regenerating treatment.

Do you offer manicures and pedicures for men too?

Yes. A men's manicure costs 28 € and a men's pedicure 38 €. Well-groomed hands aren't just for women.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, we accept payment by card as well as cash.
